01 / About

A Swiss design and technology company, since 2005.

Elysium was founded in Geneva in 2005. We design and build websites, apps, business software and 3D visuals for companies and international organisations.

Our clients include UBS, Nestlé, UEFA and Caterpillar, and the WHO, WIPO, the ILO and UNICEF. Our headquarters are in Geneva, our research team, Elysium Labs, works in Zürich, and our 3D studio is in Buenos Aires.

WHO · International organisation · Health

Websites, films and 3D for the WHO

The need
The World Health Organization needed websites, films and IT advice for its programmes.

GIA · Gemmology · Mobile

A mobile app and software for GIA

The need
GIA, which grades diamonds and other gems, needed to record where diamonds come from, and publish it.

A mobile app, and the software and publication platform behind diamond origin. The app opens on one question: the GIA report number.

CUAMM · UNICEF · Healthcare · 2022–2023

Medical records and newborn care in Sierra Leone

The need
A hospital in Freetown wanted to record patient data on computers, so its team could decide with figures in hand.
What we made
With CUAMM, a pilot of medical-record software at PMCH Hospital, Freetown, in March 2022: more than three weeks on site, ending with the hospital team trained. In May 2023, a tool for UNICEF that tracks medicine use in baby-care units in several hospitals.
Sierra Leone

06 / Private AI

Private AI servers, for organisations that cannot send confidential data to a public AI.

In preparation at Elysium Labs, Zürich: dedicated servers on which an organisation runs open-weight language models over its own documents. Prompts, files and answers stay on that server.

Model
Open-weight language models, running on the server itself. No prompt or file is sent to a public AI provider.
Answers
Drawn from the organisation’s own documents through retrieval-augmented generation, one of the Labs’ research lines.
Access
One private server per client organisation, used by its own team.
Status
In preparation. Organisations can register their interest now.
