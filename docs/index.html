04 — Private LLM infrastructure

Open-weight models, running inside your perimeter.

Elysium deploys language models on hardware the client controls — on-premise, or Swiss sovereign cloud. Research corpora, patient data and internal intelligence stay inside the perimeter and reach no third-party API.

Data residency

Weights, vector store and logs sit where you put them. We name the machine, the region and the retention period in the contract.

Regulatory fit

Built against nLPD and GDPR, and against the sector rule that actually binds you: clinical data protection, banking secrecy, or UN information classification.

No third-party training

Your corpus trains your model and nothing else. That is an architectural property here, and it survives a vendor audit.

Audit trail

Every prompt, every retrieval and every response is attributable, with the source documents a grounded answer drew from.

What we run

Model selection, GPU sizing, fine-tuning on your corpus, retrieval over your systems, evaluation, and operation afterwards.

One thing said plainly. GPT, Claude and Gemini cannot be downloaded; they are API-only, and any vendor who offers to install one on your servers is describing something that does not exist. What runs on your metal is open-weight: Llama, Mistral, Qwen, DeepSeek, gpt-oss and their fine-tunes. On general reasoning the frontier APIs are still ahead. On a bounded domain with your documents behind them, the gap closes and the data never leaves.
