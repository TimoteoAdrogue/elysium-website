01 · Deep learning · medical imaging

Applications of deep learning — an example from medicine

Medical video segmentation on cardiac ultrasound, taken from an academic setting toward potential in-hospital use — one concrete example of the deep-learning practice we take seriously.

The project focused on automatic delineation of the mitral valve across 2D echocardiography sequences — a routine imaging modality where consistent masks matter for downstream analysis and planning. Labels varied in provenance and quality, so the modelling effort had to reconcile noisier annotations with stricter clinical ground truth when it mattered; sparse annotations, region cues, and full-sequence predictions all had to hang together in one pipeline. The work sits inside a broader deep-learning practice: time spent experimenting with neural networks to build intuition about where they actually help and where they just overfit, how architectural choices shape the optimisation process, and when simpler, classical methods still outperform them. Medicine is one of the domains where that discipline matters — fluent output is not the same thing as correct output.

02 · NLP · information systems

LLM chat interfaces & RAG stacks

Retrieval-augmented pipelines that let users query client corpora in natural language, grounded in the underlying sources.

We build reusable chat-box components paired with the retrieval-augmented generation pipelines behind them: ingestion over PDFs, structured records and SaaS exports; chunking, hybrid sparse/dense retrieval and reranking; citation-style answers that trace back to source passages; and evaluation harnesses covering faithfulness, abstention and latency under token budgets. The emphasis is on a consistent interaction pattern that stays honest as corpora grow, tenancy rules change and governance requirements tighten — rather than a one-off demo that loses its edge once people actually depend on it.
